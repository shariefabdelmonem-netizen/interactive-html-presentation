03

Definition

What AI literacy means for a teacher

Understand

How it produces answers

Enough of the mechanics to explain why it sounds sure and can still be wrong.

"It predicts text. It doesn't look things up."
Judge

Whether the output is good

Checking for errors, invented facts, bias and the wrong level for your learners.

"Is this accurate, fair and right for my class?"
Decide

When to use it, and when not to

Choosing the tasks where AI helps learning, and protecting the ones where it would get in the way.

"Does this help my students think, or think for them?"
04

Understand · how a chatbot writes

It predicts. It doesn't know.

Chatbots such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude write one small piece at a time, each time picking a likely next word from patterns in their training text.

Try it: press Predict next word and watch the sentence grow.

Plausible isn't the same as true. Nothing in this process checks facts, so a confident answer can still be wrong.

    08

    Prompting · context does the work

    Anatomy of a useful prompt

    Vague
    Make a worksheet about the past simple.

    Typical result: generic sentences, the wrong level and nothing linked to your class. You spend the saved time fixing it.

    Specific
    RoleYou are an experienced EFL teacher in a boys' preparatory school.
    TaskCreate a 10-item practice worksheet on the past simple.
    ContextGrade 8, mixed ability (A2 to B1). Theme: a class trip to a museum.
    FormatItems 1–5 gap-fill, 6–10 error correction. Answer key at the end.
    LimitsUse common irregular verbs only. Flag any item you're unsure about.
    Then you review every item.

    10

    Next steps

    Start small on Monday

    01

    Pick one time-consuming task

    Levelled texts, question banks or example sentences. Try AI on it once this week.

    02

    Test it on something you know well

    Ask a question in your own subject and note exactly what it gets wrong.

    03

    Share one prompt with a colleague

    Include what worked and what you had to fix. That's how a department builds AI literacy together.
